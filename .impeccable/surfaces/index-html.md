# Surface: index.html (landing)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: anyone planning an event in CDMX (families, couples, companies). Action: Cotizar mi evento (calculator, then WhatsApp). Proof: client photographs, closed prices, inclusions, policies. Constraints: keep logo, name, motto, all content, prices and the calculator; never read as a template.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the candy stall at La Merced: real food piled high, every price hand-lettered on a fluorescent card star. It refuses the caterer default of a soft photo, centred serif and a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Stall-shade brown-black ground (#16110f); fluorescent cartulina pink #ff2e88, lime #d9ff3d and orange #ff8a1a as price stars and two drenched bands; black marker lettering (Caveat Brush) for prices and the motto; condensed poster caps (Big Shoulders Display 900) for headings; Hanken Grotesk text; square-cut photographs; the cart's tray grid as hairline cells.

STORY: The visitor sees the food and the price at once, believes a team arrives, serves and leaves, and opens the calculator to send a WhatsApp quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed abundance photograph (candy trays) under a dark foot; h1 in giant condensed caps across the lower left; lime "Cotizar mi evento" button and a ghost "Ver los carritos"; a large pink star "desde $2,250" top right linking to the calculator; a row of fluorescent price tags (four carts) along the foot.

FORM: Puesto de La Merced, candidate 3 of 7 (assigned by the roll). Raises: strict price cells (teletext), four-scale type courage (flyers), selection lights and lifts (kinetic sand). Signature interaction: hovering a cart straightens and lifts its price star; chosen calculator options light lime and lift. seed:7c47bac9738505c4
