# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Anyone organizing an event in Mexico City and its metro area, with no single dominant segment: families (birthdays, XV años, baptisms, baby showers, kids' parties), couples and planners (weddings), and companies (corporate events). They arrive comparing options, want to see the real carts, a closed price and what is included, then ask for a quote on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose
Snack Paradise rents snack carts and builds candy tables (mesas de dulces) for events: the team arrives, sets up, serves the guests with two staff per cart and takes everything away. The site must make a visitor trust the service and send a quote request; success is a WhatsApp conversation with date, venue and guest count.

## Positioning
A full-service snack bar on wheels: closed price per cart for 30 guests (from $2,250 MXN), two staff, two hours of service, set-up and pick-up included, plus custom candy tables for 30 to 300 guests.

## Operating Context
Quotes and bookings happen on WhatsApp (+52 56 5914 5042). Booking takes a 50% deposit; the rest is paid when the staff arrives. Policies cover set-up time (1.5–2 h before), night surcharge after 23:00, breakage, cancellations and delivery outside urban CDMX.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static site: index.html, styles.css, script.js, plus the assets/ kit from the design-taste-frontend skill.
- The quote calculator (event type, guests 30–300, table style, bars, extras, carts priced per guest) must stay and keep sending the selection to WhatsApp.
- Cart catalog and prices: Snacks $2,700, Dorilocos $2,400, Esquites $2,250, Choco brochetas $2,940, Charcutería $3,900 (premium $5,400), Paletas heladas $2,550, Fresas con crema $2,850, Mesa de dulces to measure.

## Brand Commitments
- Name "Snack Paradise" and its logo (the seal with the cart, img/logo-snack-paradise-web.png) stay.
- Motto "Sabores móviles, sonrisas fijas" stays.
- Colours, type and the current chocolate/caramel look are not binding (user, 2026-11-12).
- The result must never read as a template; it must look like a premium, made-for-this-client site.

## Evidence on Hand
Client photographs in img/web/ (carts, products, events, the branded cup and seal). No testimonials, client logos, review counts or press exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles
- Show the real carts and food; photographs carry the trust.
- Price and inclusions are clear before the visitor has to ask.
- Every path ends at a WhatsApp quote, the calculator first.
- Premium means made-for-this-business craft, not generic luxury.
